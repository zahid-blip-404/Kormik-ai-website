Why it matters

Every morning, a lottery. Every workday, unrecorded.

A worker in Bangladesh faces two injustices. The first is getting the work. The second is proving they did it.

How work reaches a worker

84%

of employed people in Bangladesh work informally. About 5.8 crore people.

BBS Labour Force Survey 2024

This is not the edge of Bangladesh's economy. It is most of it.

Two injustices, every day.

One

Getting the work

At dawn, at the roadside, workers wait to be picked. Some are chosen. The rest go home with nothing, and try again tomorrow.

Two

Proving they did it

Cash in hand. No slip, no name, no date. The money changes hands. The proof never does.

Without a record, a worker can't…

contest a wage

When the pay comes short, there is nothing to point to.

show their experience

Ten years of work look the same as none.

prove they were at work when they got hurt

9.6 lakh people reported a work injury or illness in one year.

BBS Labour Force Survey 2024

And the country can't count, reach or protect them.

How work reaches a worker today

Five links, from the first order to the day's work. Only the last one is left with nothing.

    issue the work.

  3. 3

    hire for the job.

  4. 4

    A broker or sardar

    puts the crew together.

  5. 5

    The worker

    is picked at dawn, paid in cash, and left with no record.
